Tips & Tricks ▶

Three Adversary Types
  • Character — Manual entry for PCs or allies. Has Evasion, HP, Stress, Hope, and Armor.
  • Enemy (SRD) — Search the database and auto-populate all stats. You can still edit it after adding.
  • Custom — Build your own adversary from scratch with full control over all fields.
Features Formatting

Write one feature per line. Use a colon (:) to split the name from the description:

Relentless (3) - Passive: Can be spotlighted 3 times
Bite - Action: Mark a Stress to attack

Everything before the first colon renders as bold gold. Everything after is regular text. No colon = bold-only line.

Editing Cards

Every card has a ✏️ button. Enemy cards open in the Custom editor so you can tweak Type, Range, Motives, and Features after importing from the SRD.

Clickable Dots

Click any dot to toggle it. Dots start full and reduce as damage is taken. Use the +/− buttons next to each stat to adjust the max on the fly.

Card Notes

Hit the 📝 button to flip a card and write private DM notes (conditions, reminders, etc). Notes persist across sessions.

Save & Load

💾 Save exports your entire session (creatures, counters, fear) as a JSON file. 📂 Load imports it back. Your campaign also auto-saves to the browser — no data is sent anywhere.

Drag & Drop

Grab any creature card and drag it to reorder. Useful for tracking initiative or grouping enemies.
